FAQ

About the numbers

Is the 35% increase guaranteed for my unit?

No. It reflects a real managed unit's average performance. Your building, floor, view, and the market that year all affect the outcome. We'll give you a realistic picture for your specific property before you decide anything.

Why did the St. Regis unit do so much better than average?

Location and building carry real weight in short-term rental demand. Beachfront, branded residences, and high-demand islands tend to command higher nightly rates and stronger occupancy than the average unit.

What if my unit underperforms the average?

We adjust pricing and strategy continuously based on real booking data for your specific unit, not a fixed assumption. If something isn't working, we change it rather than leave it as-is.

How is nightly pricing actually decided?

We track demand day by day, factoring in events, seasonality, and competing listings nearby, so pricing moves with the market instead of staying flat all year.

How can I monitor my apartment's performance?

You get dashboard access showing who booked, how much they paid, when, and for how long, at any time.

How quickly can my unit start earning?

Within 5 days if it's already furnished, or within 15 days if it needs furnishing first.

Do prices really go up during events like F1?

Yes. Pricing adjusts to demand around F1, concerts, and other major events, which is where the biggest jumps in nightly rate happen.

Does occupancy stay steady year-round?

No, it moves with seasonality and events, higher during winter, F1, and major concerts, quieter in the peak summer heat. The average figures here reflect a full year, not just the strong months.

How and when do I get paid?

Payouts are monthly, with full visibility into every booking behind the number through your dashboard.
