FAQ

Questions owners ask

Which areas does Roooms manage?

Currently Reem Island, Yas Island, Saadiyat Island, and Al Raha Beach in Abu Dhabi.

Who will furnish my apartment?

We do the furnishing, using our own team and hotel-style standard, and you cover the furnishing cost. It's a one-time investment that sets the unit up to earn from day one.

Who registers with DCT?

We handle the DCT licensing and compliance for your unit, along with insurance and the guest welcome package.

Who pays for laundry and cleaning?

The guest. Cleaning and laundry are charged per stay as part of the booking, not an ongoing cost to you.

What happens if there's damage?

We hold a deposit from guests, the apartment carries mandatory insurance, and the booking platforms themselves protect owners by deducting from the guest directly when damage is reported.

How can I monitor my apartment?

You get access to a dashboard showing who booked, how much they paid, when, and for how long, at any time.

How quickly can my unit start earning?

Within 5 days if it's already furnished, or within 15 days if it needs furnishing first.

How is this different from renting long-term?

A long-term lease is one fixed price, all year, with tenant risk and a vacancy gap every time someone moves out. Holiday-home management means the market pays more on nights it's worth more, guests are vetted per stay, and you're paid monthly with full visibility.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Talk to us directly about your specific unit. Terms are discussed per property, not standardized here.
